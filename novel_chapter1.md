# Chapter 1 | Asa Webb

<br>

“New day, new life. My name is Asa Webb, and starting today—I’ll be attending Blue Sisters High School. It’s located in Kasato, a quiet northern town in Japan. The air feels clear, the people seem joyful. It really feels like the beginning of something great,” he internally monologued.

The fast train cut through the air as it travelled to his new destination—the place where he’ll live from now on. Asa walked the busy car, steadily walking to avoid tripping. But suddenly, the train hit a hard bump that sent him flying forward. He crashed into the person in front of him, and a thump followed. He quickly steadied himself, grabbing an occupied handrail. The person he accidentally bumped into started to turn, and he feared he would get cursed at—or even worse. The guy finally turned around. He was met with an embarrassed, apologetic smile instead.

“Oh! My bad, this always happens on this specific bump!” he nervously said as to avoid any issue with him. At Asa's reassurance, he softly laughed and ran his short blonde hair back with one hand. His eyes travelled down his body, and he noticed Asa’s uniform under his blue sweater. He smiled and began speaking with excitement.

“Wait! You’re the new transfer student, right? I actually got to meet you first!” he exclaimed.

He hadn’t thought anyone would notice that, but he did. He continued, “Guess that means I’ve got a head start on being your first friend!” he said.

At his words, Asa felt a surge of energy coursing through his body. He was right, he was indeed his friend. At least he had someone before he started his first official day at Blue Sisters High School. Before Asa could speak, he saw the boy facepalm.

“Oh, I almost forgot to introduce myself. My name is Koda Sable, but you can just call me Koda.”

The two of them finally found an empty seat, and he continued, “Anyways, we’re close to school. Bummer. I hope we have classes together, we can sneak notes and stuff,” he said excitedly, but quickly blushed. “W-wait, no! That’s what crushes do, scratch that!” he stammered.

Asa couldn’t help but let out a soft, quick laugh. Koda quickly recovered.

“Whatever. Hey, why don’t we hang out after school!” he said.

Asa was surprised at how fast he was. He was already asking him to hang out. But he didn’t disagree. In fact, he wanted to hang out with him—Koda looked like an honest person. He thought about making the most out of this friendship. It was the best he could do right now—even more convenient now since he didn’t know much about Kasato.

Out the train window, Asa started to see the school between the trees as they passed by. A tall, modern school—maybe three floors by the looks of it? It was a noticeable contrast to the rural appearance of the town. Maybe an old school had been in its place, but was remodeled? Asa could only watch as the train started to brake. The pneumatic hiss signaled the train had come to a halt, and the doors slid open briefly. Students began to exit the train as more civilians began to flood the car.

“Come on, let’s go dude. Or else we’ll be absorbed by the waves of workers,” Koda said hurriedly.

They both got out of the car and into the train station. They walked about five minutes before they arrived at the school gates, students walking in from every direction—some younger, some older.

“You’re a second-year student like me, the school uniform can say a lot about you,” Koda explained as they walked to the double entry doors.

Asa felt happy, he wasn’t alone today. He had someone he could rely on. Perhaps the new school year wouldn’t be too harsh on him. They stepped onto the hallway, students walking to and from every direction.

“Welcome to Blue Sisters, Asa.”
