# Chapter 2 | Blue Sisters High School

The bell rang after the long, exhausting first day of school. People were exiting the building in waves, pushing each other. The afternoon chatter was beginning to take place. Asa carried his backpack behind his back, holding the strap tightly. As he walked closer to the gates, he heard someone yell at him from behind—it was Koda, he could tell from his voice. *He’s never afraid to have an audience*, Asa thought.

“So, how was your first day at Blue Sisters High? Pretty neat, right?” he teasingly said. But his grin dropped into a soft groan, his shoulders slumped. “Who am I kidding, homeroom is boring!” he almost yelled out the last bit.

As the two of them neared the gate, he continued. “Speaking of homeroom, we managed to get classes together!” he said, his mood going back to normal. Again, his teasing smirk returned. “Any chicks caught your fancy?” he jokingly said, leaning closer to Asa’s face.

He continued walking, swatting a nearby fly with his hand. “Anyways, we got a monster of a test coming up in a few weeks,” he said, letting out a soft groan. “If I could instead ditch school and play video games, or watch a movie at 100 Cinema… If I studied enough, maybe the teacher would stop targeting me. She really gets on my nerves,” he hissed the last part.

The two of them were finally at the gate. People poured into the pavement and crosswalks—which were busiest at this time of day, above the morning traffic. Koda continued to drone about the upcoming test, until Asa felt his pocket vibrating. He reached out a hand into it and pulled out his phone. Someone was calling him.

There was no phone number on screen, but the call answered itself.

He saw a string of corrupted characters when he entered the call with the individual on the other side. The person on the other side began speaking:

“H- hel-llo?”

“An-anyo--ne lis-t-tening!?”

“P-le-ase, som-eo-ne!”

“H-e--lp!”

The voice was glitchy, corrupted perhaps. But it sounded like a raspy, boyish voice. It sounded like it belonged to an eight-year-old. Before he could say anything, the call ended. His phone went black, and he saw his reflection on the black mirror.

Koda looked at Asa, then at his phone. He had a concerned look on his face.

“Uh, everything good, bro? We can always hang out later if you want…” he said while biting his lip, always checking on Asa.

Asa reassured Koda that it wasn’t anything serious, nothing to be concerned about. But he didn’t know who had called. Was it a prank call, something deeper?

Nonetheless, Asa decided to hang out with Koda without telling him what happened. The voice had seemed urgent, like it was trying to tell him something—but what that something was, he didn’t know.

---

Asa arrived home at nine after a thrilling hangout in the arcade. Koda couldn’t stop bragging about the amount of tickets he’d won and that were resting inside his account. Asa walked up the stairs of his apartment building, and then stood towards his door. He fetched his key from his left pocket and proceeded to open the door.

He closed the door and went straight into his bedroom to relax, throwing his backpack next to the entrance. His mind went back to that call. Who had really called him, if there was no phone number that could be traced? And even more, you needed a phone number or some kind of signal in order to make a call.

*It has to be someone in Kasato*, he thought.

But as soon as he closed his eyes to think, he comfortably fell asleep.
